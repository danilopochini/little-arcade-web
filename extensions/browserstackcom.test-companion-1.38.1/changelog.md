## [1.35.3]

### 🌟 What's New

- **Test code review**: Test Companion can now review your test changes for behaviour, not style — it flags tests that will flake, break outside your machine, don't actually assert, or ignore your repo's conventions, and shows the findings in an in-chat review card you can accept or reject.

- **Fix healed locators**: When BrowserStack self-heals broken locators during a run, clicking "Heal all Permanently" in Failure Analysis applies those heals to your source, re-runs the affected tests on BrowserStack to verify them, and presents one change for you to review.
