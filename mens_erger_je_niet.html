How to play

  1. Roll a six to bring a pawn out of your base onto its colored start space.
  2. Choose one of your highlighted pawns to move clockwise by the number rolled. Moving with a six earns another roll.
  3. Land on an opponent to send that pawn back to its base. Your own pawns block a landing space.
  4. After a full lap, enter your colored home lane. You need an exact roll to get home.
  5. Be the first to get all four pawns home to win.

Click a highlighted pawn to move it. There are no protected squares on the track.

You play red against three computer opponents.
